# Current Tend decision overrides

These documented decisions supersede the identified requirements in the
approved Kotlin-era Tend evolution specification and its associated visual
references. The earlier artifacts remain preserved as historical design
records.

- Tend is mandatory light mode. This supersedes the earlier requirement to
  follow the device's light or dark setting.
- A post-save acknowledgment has a maximum duration of three seconds. This
  supersedes the earlier requirement that it remain visible until the person's
  next action.

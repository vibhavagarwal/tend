Design system summary
Everything the chosen direction — still point, voice first — relies on. Semantic roles only; no named colours in code, no component library beyond the primitives listed here. Screens: Tend, spoken.

Colour carries interface meaning only — action, focus, confirmation, error. It never grades a habit, never marks absence, never signals volume.
02 · Typography roles
Expressive
EB Garamond · 400 / 400 italic
Headings (34), prompts (31), recap sentence (29), transcripts (28 italic), send-off (26), acknowledgment (27 italic), habit under review (44). Reversible: italic for what was heard, roman for what Tend says.
Functional
Karla · 400 / 500
Entry fields (19), list lines and totals (17), dates and helper text (16), tertiary controls (15). Never below 15. Buttons 17 regular — no all-caps.
Caption
IBM Plex Mono · 400
Section labels (11, uppercase, .14em), counts inside recap lines (15), week range and parse line (11–12). The only place letter-spacing is used.
Device text scaling to 200% is supported: every block is flow-sized, nothing is height-capped, and the composer keeps its space by pushing history down rather than clipping.
03 · Spacing
Base unit 6. Scale 6 · 12 · 14 · 22 · 26 · 34 · 52. Screen padding 16 top, 26 sides, 20 bottom. Header to wordmark 14. Label to display line 14–16. Display line to primary action 22–26. Centred rule 56 wide with 30–34 above and 20–22 below. History lines 14 apart. Held space above and below the composer is elastic, not fixed — it absorbs text scaling first.
04 · Shape and elevation
Actions are full pills (999). App icon 11. Screen corners 36. No cards, no panels, no shadows anywhere in the app — the only shadow in the mockups belongs to the device bezel. Structure comes from 1px hairlines, the 56px rule, and space. Focused text field: 1px accent underline, no fill.
05 · Action hierarchy
Hold to speak
Primary — 60px filled pill. Exactly one per state: Hold to speak, or Save activity once something has been heard. Full width of the content column.
Reflect
← Track
Secondary — 44px outlined pill. Navigation and stop: Reflect, Back, Done. Accent outline when it is the way forward, hairline outline when it is a return.
Edit as text Cancel
Tertiary — 44px text. Accent for alternative paths (Type instead, Edit as text, New habit); muted for retreat (Cancel, Not now, Say it again). No underline, so links and controls stay distinguishable.
Reflect has no primary action at all — it is read-only, and its only controls are Back and the system back gesture. No bottom navigation anywhere.
06 · Motion
Five 3px level bars are the only continuous movement in the app; they follow microphone level and settle the instant listening stops. Acknowledgment: 200ms fade with a 4px rise, then it stays until the next action — no dismissal, no second confirmation. Track ↔ Reflect: 220ms cross-fade, reversed on Back. New history line: 160ms fade, list does not animate its reflow. Transcript text replaces itself with no typing effect. Nothing loops, nothing delays a save, nothing celebrates. Reduced motion: all transitions become instant and the bars hold at rest while the word “Listening” carries the state.
07 · Voice, copy and recap rules
Four states on one screen: ready → listening → heard → saved. Speech creates habits (“I would like to track swimming”) and entries (“I swam for 20 minutes yesterday”); the Activity Date is resolved from the sentence and shown in words before saving, with the raw phrase printed beneath. Text is the correction path and the recognition-failure path. Acknowledgments come from a fixed local corpus of 24 messages rotated globally, never repeating until exhausted, never naming the habit, duration, momentum or the person. Habit creation returns the fixed send-off instead. Reflect reports frequency only, Monday–Sunday by Activity Date, no zero-count habits, archived habits last and marked, sentence form up to three habits and a list at four or more.
Outside this system
Streaks, targets, scores, charts, dashboard cards, recap notifications, unread badges or review-due prompts, garden or wilting imagery, in-app theme switcher, wallpaper/dynamic colour, bottom navigation, accounts, cloud sync, shared data, and any LLM or network call in Reflect or in the acknowledgment corpus.

# 04: Make Habit Entry review editable while retaining Entry Safety

**What to build:** Before a Habit Entry is saved, Tend shows the verbatim Habit Statement and a clear structured reading, including the plain-language Activity Date and source date wording. A person can correct the proposal or speak again, while ambiguity clarification, duplicate handling, application validation, and explicit confirmation continue to protect the record.

**Blocked by:** 03: Introduce the in-place voice-first composer.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A reviewed Habit Entry shows only recognized details, never invents duration or quantity, and requires explicit Save after any correction.
- [ ] Ambiguous, unmatched, invalid, canceled, duplicate, and retry-ready proposals remain safe and understandable, with no automatic Habit Entry write.
- [ ] Gateway and local interpretation paths preserve the review information needed for the UI, including source date wording, with automated contract and UI coverage.

Voice model
Four states, one screen: ready → listening → heard → saved. Nothing pops over; the composer area simply changes what it holds.
“I would like to track swimming” creates the habit and returns the fixed send-off. “I swam for 20 minutes yesterday” saves an entry against Swimming, with the Activity Date resolved from the sentence and shown as plain words you can read before saving.
Everything heard is shown as text, and the parsed reading is printed under it, so nothing depends on audio alone. Duration is kept on the entry and never enters Reflect, which still counts entries only.
If speech isn't recognised, the text path opens with whatever was heard already filled in — that's the error path, not encouragement.
Logo in place
App icon sits in the header slot at 40px with an 11px radius; the wordmark centres beneath it at roughly 34px ink height, clear space equal to the height of the T.
Interface accent moved to the icon's teal so buttons and the mark agree.
Dark theme currently knocks the wordmark out to a single tone — a light lockup that keeps the green stem would be better; send one and I'll swap it in.
The wordmark drops to 45% while listening so the transcript is the only thing with weight.
Motion and access
The five level bars are the only continuous movement in the app; they follow input level and stop the moment listening stops. Under reduced motion they hold at rest and the word “Listening” carries the state.
Transcript text replaces itself without animation — no typing effect. Acknowledgment fades in over 200ms with a 4px rise and stays until the next action.
Speak and Save are 60px tall, secondary controls 44px; body text 16–19px, transcripts 25–28px.
